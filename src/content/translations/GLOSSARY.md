# Translation glossary

How the blog's translations render Bounda's vocabulary, so every post says the same thing the same
way. Taken from the posts already published; a new term is added here the first time a post uses
it. Code, identifiers, file names and error codes are never translated.

Spanish is reviewed by the author. Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese are machine translations,
checked before publishing by translating them back to English and comparing them with the
original, and labelled as such on the page, with a link to report a mistake.

Notes:

- **Do not add** what the original does not say: no local laws, examples or asides. A translation
  that needs context for its readers gets it from the author, in the original.
- Link texts that point to English pages stay in English, followed by "(in English)" in the
  language of the page when that helps: (en inglés), (英語), (em inglês).
- pt-BR uses Brazilian conventions: “ ” quotes, "você", the subjunctive after *sempre que*.
- A scene of the demo keeps its English name, with a short gloss in parentheses.
